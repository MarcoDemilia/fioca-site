Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Fioca is a macOS app that dims everything on your screen except the apps you choose.

The short version: Fioca collects no data. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What Fioca uses, and where it stays

  • Window positions. While a session runs, Fioca reads where windows are on your screen, so it can keep your chosen apps bright and dim the rest. It does not read window titles or content. This happens on your Mac and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Fioca remembers the apps you picked last time, your session length, your dim level and whether you've seen the intro. These are saved only on your Mac, inside Fioca's own sandbox.
  • Notifications. If you allow it, Fioca shows a local notification when a session ends. It doesn't use push notifications or any server.

What Fioca does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No internet connection
  • No Screen Recording or Accessibility access
  • No sharing or selling of data, to anyone

Children

Fioca collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
