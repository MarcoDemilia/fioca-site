Getting started

  1. Click the candle in the menu bar and choose Start Fioca…
  2. Pick up to 4 apps you need right now
  3. Choose how long, then click Start

Common questions

Where is Fioca?
It lives in the menu bar (top right of your screen), not in the Dock. Look for the candle.
I can't click an app.
That's on purpose: apps you didn't pick are dimmed and blocked during a session. Your picked apps always stay clickable.
How do I end a session early?
Click the candle → End session, type “I want to end my session”, then click End session.
Can I change the dim level during a session?
No — it's locked once you start, so you can't tweak your way out of focus. Choose it in the picker before you press Start; you'll see a live preview while you drag the slider.
I don't get the “welcome back” notification.
Open System Settings → Notifications → Fioca and turn on Allow notifications. If they're off, Fioca shows its own small message instead.
Does Fioca need special permissions?
No. It doesn't need Screen Recording or Accessibility access, and it collects no data.
