Fioca

Keep what matters.
Dim the rest.

Pick up to 4 apps, set a time, and everything else fades into the background.

In Italian, fioca means dim — like a soft, low light. That's what Fioca does to everything you don't need right now.

  1. Click the candle in the menu bar and choose Start Fioca…
  2. Pick the apps you need
  3. Choose how long, then let go

When time's up, everything comes back exactly as you left it.

For macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Collects no data.
